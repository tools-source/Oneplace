Information We Collect OnePlace may collect the following information when you use the app: Account information (such as email address) when you sign in using: Sign in with Apple Google Sign-In Data you create inside the app (such as tasks, finance entries, split expenses, organizer data, and communication board content) This information is used only to provide app functionality and synchronize your data across your devices. You can review: Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ Data Sharing OnePlace does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is only used to provide core app functionality. Data Retention Your data remains stored in your account until you delete it. If you delete your account, associated data may be permanently removed from our systems. Children’s Privacy OnePlace is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action. Security We use industry-standard encryption and secure authentication methods to protect your information. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
