Information We Collect OnePlace may collect the following information when you use the app: Account and sign-in information, such as your email address, name, unique user ID, and sign-in provider, when you sign in using: Sign in with Apple Google Sign-In Data you create inside the app, such as: Finance entries Organizer tasks, notes, due dates, and reminders Flow items, including bills, income items, due dates, recurrence details, and reminder settings Split people, balances, and shared expenses Talk card content, including titles, phrases, emojis, and audio recordings you choose to save Text or voice commands you submit when using search, AI-assisted input, voice input, Siri, or App Shortcuts Permissions and Device Features OnePlace may request access to certain device features if you choose to use them, such as: Notifications, to send reminders and alerts you enable in the app Your microphone, to capture voice input for AI commands, Talk features, and other voice-enabled actions Speech recognition, to convert your spoken words into text so the app can process voice commands Siri and App Shortcuts, to let you launch supported actions hands-free from your device Your photo library, if image-based communication features are available in your version of the app and you choose to use them How We Use Information We use this information only to provide and improve app functionality, authenticate your account, interpret your requests, schedule reminders, synchronize your data across your devices, and support hands-free features such as voice input and Siri shortcuts. AI-Assisted Features When you use AI-assisted features, OnePlace may send the text of your typed or spoken request to a third-party AI service so the app can interpret what you want to do and return a structured result for use inside the app. For example, OnePlace may process requests to classify a finance entry, understand a natural-language command, or turn a spoken request into an in-app action. Only the content needed to process your request is sent for that purpose. How Your Data Is Stored Your data is stored using: Firebase Authentication (for login) Cloud Firestore (for cloud data storage and sync) Some app preferences, cached state, and other local app data may also be stored on your device to support app functionality. Data is transmitted using encrypted connections, such as HTTPS/TLS. OnePlace does not sell your personal data or use your data for advertising.
