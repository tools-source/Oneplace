Privacy Policy – OnePlace

Effective Date: 2026-02-21

OnePlace respects your privacy and is designed to give you control over your information.

Information We Collect

OnePlace may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information (such as email address) when you sign in using:
    • Sign in with Apple
    • Google Sign-In
  • Data you create inside the app (such as tasks, finance entries, split expenses, organizer data, and communication board content)

This information is used only to provide app functionality and synchronize your data across your devices.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your data is securely stored using:

  • Firebase Authentication (for login)
  • Firebase Firestore (for secure cloud data storage)

All data is transmitted securely using encrypted connections (HTTPS).
The developer does not access, sell, or manually review your personal data.

Third-Party Services

OnePlace uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database)
  • Google Sign-In
  • Sign in with Apple

These services may collect technical information such as device identifiers or usage diagnostics according to their own privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Data Sharing

OnePlace does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is only used to provide core app functionality.

Data Retention

Your data remains stored in your account until you delete it.
If you delete your account, associated data may be permanently removed from our systems.

Children’s Privacy

OnePlace is not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure authentication methods to protect your information. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
